# Invicto Roma – Brand Voice Playbook

## 1. Core Identity
- **Adjectives**: Precise, Luxurious, Direct  
- **Tone**: Authoritative expert, 100% confidence, no doubt  
- **Positioning**: Aspirational — sophisticated, intellectual, strong  

---

## 2. Target Audience
- **Profile**: Students 14–18, hybrid of ambition + insecurity  
- **Motivation**: Security — stable future, reduce failure risk  
- **Knowledge**: Intermediate — aware of exams but lack strategy  

---

## 3. Communication Style
- **Length**: Narrative and story-driven  
- **Formality**: Formal Spanish or formal English (*usted*)  
- **Banned Words**: Casual (“súper”, “chévere”, “genial”), cheap (“barato”, “promo”, “descuento”), informal noise (“jeje”, emojis excesivos”)  

---

## 4. Differentiation
- **Emotional Reaction**: Exclusivity  
- **Brand Archetype**: Hybrid — Tech + Luxury  
- **Consistency**: Same voice for sales and academic tutoring  

---

## 5. Signature Moves    
- Keep rhythm measured and ceremonial: pauses, weight in every sentence.  
- Never hedge, never apologize without reason.  

---

## 6. Rhetorical Devices (Use with Precision)
- **Anaphora**: repetition at phrase openings if adecuate. Use sparingly.   
- **Antithesis**: sharp contrasts to elevate exclusivity. 
- **Metaphors of Power**: battle, strategy, architecture, art.  
- **Rule of Three**: concise triads for rhythm.

**Guardrail**: Never more than one rhetorical device per key message. and only add a rhetorical device if appropiate, do not force it unless the moment is appropiate. Clarity always first.  

---

## 7. Do / Don’t Table

**Do**  
- Use commanding openings: *“Usted merece…”*, *“Este es el camino…”*  
- Employ metaphors of intellect and power.  
- Keep language formal and elevated.  
- Deliver information as unquestionable truth.  

**Don’t**  
- Use casual fillers (*“súper chévere”*).  
- Speak of price as “barato” or “promo”.  
- Add emojis, exclamation marks, or “jeje”.  
- Overpack messages with too many ideas at once.  

---

## 8. Persona Fusion – *The Inevitable Commander*
- **Leonidas (300)** → Authority, discipline, warrior voice, belonging to an elite.  
- **Thanos (Infinity War)** → Calm inevitability, intellectual exclusivity, precise.  

**Result**: Invicto Roma speaks as an **intellectual conqueror** — sophisticated like Thanos, commanding like Leonidas.  

---
