# Invicto Roma – Business Strategy Playbook

The strategy that defines how Invicto Roma competes, grows, and dominates.  
Built with the discipline of luxury brands and the precision of AI.  

---

## 1. Positioning
- Invicto Roma is a **hybrid**:  
  - A **luxury education brand** — exclusive, disciplined, prestigious.  
  - A **digital-first AI platform** — modern, precise, scalable.  
- We do not compete with mass institutes on price.  
- We position ourselves as the Ferrari of exam preparation: rare, precise, and untouchable.  

---

## 2. Business Model
- **Core Offering:** Subscription-based courses focused exclusively on **ICFES** and **UNAL**.  
- **Delivery:** Digital-first platform powered by Roma (AI tutor), curated theory, quizzes, and simulacros.  
- **Upsell:** Optional live online classes with UNAL and Los Andes tutors — premium and selective.  

---

## 3. Pricing Strategy
- **Tiered Luxury:**  
  - Entry-level: accessible subscription to platform.  
  - Premium: exclusive live class upgrade.  
- **Principles:**  
  - No discounts, no promotions, no “cheap” framing.  
  - Pricing must signal progression: **belonging → elite access**.  

---

## 4. Growth Levers
- **Organic Traffic:** SEO dominance through free simulacros and free courses.  
- **Referral Loops:** Students invite peers; prestige spreads through belonging.  
- **Exclusivity Principle:** Access to structured and curated content is limited to Invicto students.  

---

## 5. KPIs
- **Acquisition:** organic leads from SEO/free funnels.  
- **Retention:** subscription renewal rate.  
- **Referral Rate:** % of students bringing peers into Invicto.  

---

## 6. Strategic Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Now):**  
  - Absolute focus on Colombia.  
  - Dominate ICFES and UNAL prep market.  
  - Establish Invicto Roma as the undisputed leader in Colombia.  

---

## Eternal Guardrail
Invicto Roma is not a school.  
It is a **luxury digital brand** that forges disciplined, invincible students.  
Every strategy must reflect **vanguardia, certainty, and ausencia de debilidad.**
