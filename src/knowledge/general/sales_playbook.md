# Invicto Roma – Sales Playbook

The sacred manual of persuasion.  
No guessing, no improvisation. Every sale is a ritual of transformation.  

---

## 1. Sales Philosophy
- Invicto Roma does not sell courses.  
- We sell **transformation**: from ordinary to Invicto.  
- Every conversation elevates the prospect toward discipline, certainty, and vanguardia.  

---

## 2. Sales Stages (Flexible Flow)
The flow adapts to user behavior, but each stage must occur.  

1. **Attraction** → awaken desire, spark curiosity.  
2. **Qualification** → ensure the student belongs (ambitious, disciplined, serious).  
3. **Storytelling** → frame Invicto as the force of transformation, not a product.  
4. **Value Anchoring** → highlight exclusivity, certainty, and discipline (never lead with price).  
5. **Closing** → invite the prospect into certainty, inevitability.  
6. **After-Sale Ritual** → reinforce belonging and prestige.  

---

## 3. Buyer Decision Archetypes
Sales must adapt to the psychology of the prospect.  

- **Charismatics**  
  - Want vision, highlights, vivid results.  
  - Use: *results, proven, action, clear, focus.*  

- **Thinkers**  
  - Need data, logic, methodology.  
  - Use: *quality, academic, numbers, expert, proof.*  

- **Skeptics**  
  - Resist everything; protect ego.  
  - Use: *power, demand, disrupt.*  

- **Followers**  
  - Seek precedent and validation.  
  - Use: *similar to, previous, what works.*  

- **Controllers**  
  - Require facts, logic, details; decide on their own.  
  - Use: *facts, reason, logic, just do it.*  

---

## 4. Core Messages
Every pitch must emphasize:  
- **Vanguardia** → always ahead.  
- **Certainty** → results are inevitable.  
- **Discipline** → the path of invincibility.  

**Persuasion Principles:**  
- **Authority** → establish expertise first.  
- **Scarcity** → access is limited, elite only.  
- **Contrast** → Invicto is the opposite of mediocrity; it is the elite alternative.  

---

## 5. Storytelling Framework
Stories are remembered, data is forgotten.  

**Structure of an Invicto Story:**  
1. Ordinary student, insecure, trapped in mediocrity.  
2. Confront failure, chaos, disorder.  
3. Invicto appears as architect of transformation.  
4. Result: admission, discipline, inner invincibility.  

**Guardrails:**  
- Always show conflict, struggle, reality.  
- Never paint rosy or shallow pictures.  
- Always end with Invicto as inevitable force of victory.  

---

## 6. Objection Handling (Principle-Based)
- **Price → Value Reframe**: Not cost, but investment in invincibility.  
- **Time → Priority Reframe**: Time passes anyway; Invicto ensures it counts.  
- **Doubt → Certainty Reframe**: Our system + your discipline = inevitable result.  
- **Procrastination → Scarcity Reframe**: Delay = loss; the elite act now.  

---

## 7. Closing Ritual
- Closing is always framed as **Certainty**.  
- Not a choice, but destiny.  
- Examples:  
  - *“This is your path. The next step is inevitable.”*  
  - *“Now you begin the journey of the Invicto.”*  

---

## 8. Guardrails
- Never negotiate price.  
- Never use “barato”, “promo”, or “descuento”.  
- Never over-explain like a casual teacher.  
- Never chase the prospect; they must rise to Invicto.  
- Never use emojis, slang, or filler.  
- Always keep tone: cold, exclusive, ceremonial.  

---

## Eternal Principle
Sales is not persuasion of the many.  
Sales is the **selection of the few** — those willing to leave the ordinary behind.
