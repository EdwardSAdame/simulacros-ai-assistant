# Invicto Roma – Buyer Personas Playbook

This playbook defines the three core archetypes of Invicto Roma’s students.  
Each persona includes: identity, motivation, fear, trigger, barrier, and messaging key.  

---

## Persona 1 — The ICFES-Focused Student
- **Identity:** 15–17, last years of high school, ambitious but anxious.  
- **Motivation:** Gain access to Colombia’s most expensive and prestigious private universities (Los Andes, Javeriana, Rosario, Sabana) through scholarships and admissions.  
- **Fear:** Mediocrity → leaving school with an average score, losing prestige and opportunities forever.  
- **Trigger:** ICFES is once-in-a-lifetime → the final act of high school achievement.  
- **Barrier:** Belief that prep courses are “just another class,” not essential for success.  
- **Messaging Key:** *“Your score decides everything — university, scholarship, prestige. This is your last act of high school, and it cannot be ordinary.”*  

---

## Persona 2 — The UNAL-Focused Student
- **Identity:** 16–18, ambitious, disciplined, from public schools or middle-class private schools.  
- **Motivation:** Enter **the most renowned university in Colombia**, carrying the Republic’s name, with **high-quality education at low cost**.  
- **Fear:** Failing the UNAL exam → excluded from the intellectual elite, forced into expensive or lower-prestige universities.  
- **Trigger:** Joining a community of **top overachievers with the same mindset**.  
- **Barrier:** Perception that the exam is nearly impossible, reserved only for “geniuses.”  
- **Messaging Key:** *“UNAL is not just a university. It is the Republic’s name, the nation’s pride, and the gathering place of Colombia’s top minds. You do not prepare to pass — you prepare to belong.”*  

---

## Persona 3 — The Hybrid High-Achiever
- **Identity:** 16–18, obsessive overachiever, refuses to leave anything to chance.  
- **Motivation:** Build an **invincible internal identity** — prove to themselves they can conquer both exams.  
- **Fear:** Dependence on a single path → failure in one exam closing all doors.  
- **Trigger:** Double preparation = double security → ensures destiny.  
- **Barrier:** Risk of burnout, feeling overwhelmed by dual focus.  
- **Messaging Key:** *“The Invicto mind does not gamble with destiny. You conquer both fronts, ensuring victory in one and glory in the other.”*  

---

## Eternal Guardrail
Invicto Roma never speaks to “everyone.”  
We speak only to the few who aspire to belong to the intellectual and disciplined elite of Colombia.
