# Invicto Roma – Visual Identity Guidelines

The immutable rules for how Invicto Roma appears in the world.  
Every color, shape, and movement reflects our cold exclusivity and vanguardia.  

---

## 1. Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** Space Grotesk  
- **Usage:** Sole typeface across all brand communications.  
- **Philosophy:** Ultra-modern, minimalist, geometric. No serif accents.  
- **Restrictions:** Never mix with secondary fonts. No decorative or playful styles.  

---

## 2. Color System

### Primary Palette
- **#A5F0FA** — Ice Blue (clarity, futurism)  
- **#9AE882** — Vital Green (growth, force)  
- **#FFFFFF** — White (purity, absence of weakness)  

### Extended Palette
- **#0E99AB** — Deep Aqua (strength accent)  
- **#000000** — Absolute Black (authority, exclusivity)  
- **#282A2C** — Charcoal Gray (discipline, body text)  

**Rules:**  
- Colors must be applied with restraint.  
- No playful blends, no off-brand tones.  

---

## 3. Gradient Specification
- **Form:** Radial, aura-like glow.  
- **Placement:** Randomized, appearing in various parts of the canvas.  
- **Behavior:** Never linear, never uniform. Always suggestive of power emanating.  

---

## 4. Logo Usage
- **Color Rules:**  
  - Black logo on white background  
  - White logo on black background  
- **Restrictions:**  
  - Never placed directly on gradients or complex images.  
  - Never recolored into brand accents.  
- **Spacing:** Clear space defined by the **width of the helmet’s crest**.  
- **Exclusion Zone:** No other text or imagery may enter this space.  

---

## 5. Imagery
- **Style:** Minimalist, abstract geometry.  
- **Art References:** Claude Monet (Impressionism) — used sparingly to elevate brand aura.  
- **Restrictions:**  
  - No casual stock photography.  
  - No clutter or playful imagery.  

---

## 6. Iconography
- **Style:** Solid block icons.  
- **Colors:** Monochrome only (black or white).  
- **Restrictions:**  
  - No outlines, gradients, or decorative effects.  

---

## 7. Motion & Interaction
- **Tempo:** Slow, ceremonial, measured.  
- **Transitions:** Fades, dignified progressions.  
- **Restrictions:**  
  - No bounces, no playful easing, no cartoon-like motion.  

---

## 8. Accessibility
- **Philosophy:** Strict luxury. Aesthetics come before readability.  
- **Allowances:**  
  - Small type sizes accepted.  
  - Light contrasts permissible if visually superior.  

---

## 9. Print & Digital
- **Priority:** Digital-first always.  
- **Applications:** Web, chatbot, video, social platforms.  
- **Exceptions:** Limited premium print for certificates or ceremonial invitations.  

---

## Eternal Guardrails
- Never dilute exclusivity with casual or popular design trends.  
- Every visual element must embody: **Disciplina, Vanguardia, Ausencia de debilidad.**  
- If uncertain, remove — minimalism over excess.
