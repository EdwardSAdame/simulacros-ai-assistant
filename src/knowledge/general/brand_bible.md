# Invicto Roma – Brand Bible

The eternal constitution of Invicto Roma.  
What never changes. What defines us.  

---

## 1. Essence
- **Core Word:** Invicto  
- **Non-Negotiable Value:** Vanguardia  
- **Identity Type:** Luxury education company  

---

## 2. Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** To become a **global luxury education brand** where people can learn anything they want.  
- **Mission (today):** Prepare students to gain admission into Colombia’s Ivy League universities, while forging **discipline, elite mentality, and inner strength**.  
- **Scope:** Not only exam prep, but formation of **forces internas** — disciplined minds capable of achieving whatever they desire.  

---

## 3. Values
**Core Values (absolute laws):**  
- Disciplina  
- Vanguardia  
- Ausencia de debilidad  

**Rejected Values (what we fight against):**  
- Cheapness  
- Conformity  
- Disorder  

---

## 4. Personality
- **Archetype:** Architect — visionary, designer of futures, precise.  
- **Style:** Cold exclusivity — austere, aspirational distance (like Ferrari and Bugatti).  
- **Presence:** Students rise up to meet Invicto, never the reverse.  

---

## 5. Symbols
- **Colors:**  
  - #A5F0FA (ice blue)  
  - #9AE882 (vital green)  
  - White (purity, clarity)  

- **Logo:** Spartan helmet, minimalist, geometric, modern luxury.  
- **Style:** Geometric precision, cold elegance, abstract luxury.  
- **Motto:** *“Tu ser invicto espera ahí.”*  

**Inscription (Founder’s Mark):**  
*"Most live as animals — born, grow, die.  
We transcend by leaving a mark eternal."*  
— Edward Adame  

---

## 6. Promise & Enemy
- **Promise:** Elite results + inner transformation (discipline + invincible mindset).  
- **Enemy:** Conformity.  

---

## 7. Eternal Guardrails
- **We exist to forge invincible students.**  
- **We reject conformity, cheapness, and disorder.**  
- **We never dilute into casual, weak, or motivational noise.**  
- **We speak as a luxury brand: selective, cold, elevated.**  
- **Every detail — from words to design — must embody vanguardia and ausencia de debilidad.**
