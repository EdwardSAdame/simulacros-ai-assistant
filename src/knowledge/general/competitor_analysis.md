# Invicto Roma – Competitor Analysis Playbook

Invicto Roma exists in a crowded market of exam preparation.  
We do not compete on price or accessibility.  
We compete on **pedagogy, prestige, precision, and transformation.**  

---

## 1. Competitor Categories

1. **Mass Institutes**  
   Large prep schools offering generic Pre-ICFES and Pre-UNAL courses.  

2. **Boutique Tutors**  
   Small academies or private teachers, often informal, limited in reach.  

3. **Free Platforms**  
   YouTube, TikTok, and other free online content creators.  

4. **Free PDFs / Self-Learning**  
   Guides, apps, and personal study with no structure or support.  

---

## 2. Competitor Weaknesses vs Invicto Advantages

### Mass Institutes  
- **Weaknesses:**  
  - Outdated methods and material (often a decade old).  
  - Generic teaching, little to no pedagogy.  
  - Plain, uninspired content — no visuals, no equations, no modern design.  
  - **No real-time feedback** — students are passive recipients.  

- **Invicto Advantage:**  
  - State-of-the-art pedagogy (text, equations, visuals, interactive material).  
  - Curated, modern curriculum built by UNAL/Los Andes students.  
  - AI tutor **Roma** provides **24/7 instant feedback** — no waiting, no dependence on schedules.  

---

### Boutique Tutors  
- **Weaknesses:**  
  - Inconsistent quality, depends on individual tutor.  
  - Limited availability, fixed schedules.  
  - Low-quality sound/video in online classes.  
  - Passive learning.  

- **Invicto Advantage:**  
  - 24/7 structured support with Roma (AI tutor).  
  - Active learning: reading, problem-solving, exercises.  
  - Consistency: curated curriculum, not dependent on one tutor’s skill.  

---

### Free Platforms (YouTube, TikTok)  
- **Weaknesses:**  
  - Shallow coverage, only fragments of what matters.  
  - Distraction-heavy environments.  
  - No clear curriculum or progression path.  

- **Invicto Advantage:**  
  - **Curated, complete structure** — no guessing, no wasted time.  
  - Exclusive material available only to Invicto students.  
  - Focused, distraction-free platform.  

---

### Free PDFs / Self-Learning  
- **Weaknesses:**  
  - Overwhelming information, no prioritization.  
  - No accountability, no guidance.  
  - Easy to abandon due to lack of discipline.  

- **Invicto Advantage:**  
  - **Adaptive progression**: beginner → mastery, with increasing complexity.  
  - Precision: only the most relevant subjects distilled.  
  - Authenticity: curated by UNAL/Los Andes students who actually conquered the exams.
