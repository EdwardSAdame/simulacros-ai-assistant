# Invicto Roma – Strategic Playbooks Index

This index lists all core playbooks of Invicto Roma.  
Each defines a different dimension of the brand.  

---

## 1. brand_voice.md
Defines how Invicto Roma speaks.  
- Tone, archetypes, rhetorical devices, lexicon.  
- The voice of Roma: cold, precise, exclusive.  

---

## 2. brand_bible.md
The eternal constitution of the brand.  
- Essence, vision, mission, values, personality, symbols, promise, enemy.  
- What never changes.  

---

## 3. visual_identity.md
Guidelines for how Invicto Roma looks.  
- Typography (Space Grotesk).  
- Color palette, gradients, logo usage, spacing.  
- Imagery, iconography, motion, accessibility, digital-first rules.  

---

## 4. sales_playbook.md
The ritual of persuasion.  
- Sales philosophy, stages, buyer archetypes.  
- Core messages, storytelling framework, objection handling.  
- Closing rituals and strict guardrails.  

---

## 5. buyer_personas.md
Profiles of Invicto Roma’s three core student archetypes.  
- ICFES-focused student.  
- UNAL-focused student.  
- Hybrid high-achiever.  
- Each persona defined by identity, motivation, fear, trigger, barrier, messaging key.  

---

## 6. competitor_analysis.md
Defines the battlefield.  
- Competitor categories: mass institutes, boutique tutors, free platforms, self-learning.  
- Their weaknesses vs Invicto’s strengths.  
- Eternal differentiation: luxury, digital-first, AI precision.  

---

## 7. business_strategy.md
The master plan for growth.  
- Positioning: hybrid luxury education + digital-first AI.  
- Business model: subscription + premium upsell.  
- Pricing: tiered luxury.  
- Growth levers: organic SEO, referrals.  
- KPIs: acquisition, retention, referrals.  
- Roadmap: dominate Colombia’s ICFES + UNAL market.  

---

## 8. internal_kpis.md
Operational discipline.  
- Internal principles: luxury discipline, precision, fewer but perfect moves.  
- KPIs: AI tutor engagement, content performance, sales performance.  
- Monthly reporting rituals.  
- Hybrid SOPs: strict where critical, flexible where creative.  
- Continuous improvement: data + selective feedback.  

---

## Eternal Guardrail
These playbooks are indivisible.  
Together, they form the **Invicto Roma Codex**.  
Every action — branding, sales, operations — must align with them.
